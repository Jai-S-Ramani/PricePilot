ARCHITECTURE

How Price Intelligence Operates

Three streamlined stages from raw product URL to automated price-drop capture.

01

Paste Amazon Link or ASIN

Input any Amazon product URL or ASIN. The scraper rotates User-Agents and parses multi-region DOMs extracting live pricing, ratings, and stock status.

02

Set Target Price & Daemon Sync

Define your alert threshold in your preferred currency. The background daemon scheduler automatically executes scheduled sweeps every 30 minutes.

03

Capture Drops & Multi-Store Arbitrage

Receive rich HTML alert emails the moment prices cross below your threshold, and benchmark prices across 18 leading competitor marketplaces.
